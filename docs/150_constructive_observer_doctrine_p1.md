## 1. Why P1 is separate from P0

P0 separated observer support from existence, path persistence from identity,
fixed family extension from genuine refinement, and productive language from
completed infinity. That separation is now executable and reviewed. It was
deliberately conservative: constructibility remains open, no scoped object is
established, and all-depth/completion claims require stronger sources.

P1 asks the next question:

> Which positive constructions are sufficient to move one explicitly scoped
> judgment from OPEN to ESTABLISHED without smuggling in primitive identity,
> arbitrary observer choice, or completed infinity?

The answer is not one master axiom. Four finite-formation obligations and two
infinity obligations must stay separately checkable.

## 3. One architecture, two tracks

The six questions share source binding but do not share conclusions.

### Track A — finite formation

```text
endogenous discriminating mode
        ↓ formal shadow/admission
typed observer ──translation/refinement──> observer doctrine
        ↓
replayed construction + bound persistence + bound confluence
        ↓
finite scoped-formation judgment
```

### Track B — infinity bridge

```text
bounded windows
   ⇏ productive process
   ⇏ all-depth family
   ⇏ completed carrier (separate PΩ)
```

The arrows marked `⇏` are permanent non-implications unless the next level has
its own named source. Track A does not create infinity; Track B does not create
an observer or an object merely by being infinite.

## 4. Ontic becoming of an observer

### 4.1 Constitutive proposal

An observer is not initially a person, viewpoint, function, or test. P1 treats
observerhood as a **role acquired by a persistent discriminating mode**.

A process occupies that role relative to a coupling scope when:

1. a finite replayable genealogy begins at primitive `rez/nod/tact/breath`
   seeds and reaches structural closure before any observer admission;
2. it preserves enough recurrence to participate again;
3. coupling can leave at least two distinguishable response residues over the
   declared calibration scope;
4. those residues can condition later transitions of the process.

This is stronger than “a function returned a value.” A fixed realization map
then produces its typed R11 shadow. Distinction becomes observation when a
recurrent process retains a coupling-dependent difference and makes it operative.

### 4.2 Three questions kept separate

- **Ontic becoming:** how a discriminating recurrent role arises.
- **Formal admission:** why a closed typed observer belongs to doctrine `D`.
- **Epistemic selection:** why investigators selected this observer and scope.

A source digest addresses immutability. A prior public ledger or target-blind
protocol may address selection provenance. Neither proves ontic becoming. A
replayed genesis trace is evidence for a formal shadow of becoming, not proof
that a physical observer exists.

### 4.3 Self-observation without a final observer

An admitted observer can be encoded as input to another coupling. This produces
a new scoped observation; it does not confer absolute certification on the
first observer. Regress stops because admission is a finite doctrine obligation,
not because a privileged last observer is postulated.

## 5. Genuine translation and refinement

P0 implemented only fixed observer-family extension. P1 reserves
**refinement** for a stronger diagram.

Let a fine observer produce response `r_f`, a coarse observer response `r_c`,
and a declared partial translation turn fine responses into coarse responses.
Set the comparison domain `C = Dom(fine) ∩ Dom(coarse)`. Within a scope:

1. **information refinement** holds when translated fine responses reproduce
   coarse responses exactly for every input in `C`;
2. **domain coverage** holds when every coarse-defined presentation is also
   fine-defined;
3. **strong refinement** requires both;
4. preservation without reflection is explicitly lossy;
5. absent translation evidence stays `ABSENT`/OPEN — the evidence-absence
   sense, not checked exclusion (`reference/silence-status-map.md`); relation
   incomparability
   requires two distinct crossing witnesses refuting both inclusions.

Admitted translations require identity and composition laws carrying the same
domain, doctrine, and source bindings. Strong refinement extends the exact
factorization from `C` to the whole coarse domain. Family extension remains
non-refinement.

The first executable cases are now P1-A structural R11 projection and P1-A2
exact finite relation replay. A2 independently derives preservation, reflection,
and domain equality over every ordered pair, then classifies scoped equivalence,
strict refinement/coarsening, or OPEN and separates structural loss from proposal
conflict. Unary `Silence/Pulse` R11 partitions are nested/injective, so runtime
crossed-partition incomparability remains deferred although its classifier truth
table is tested. Structural invertibility remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

The separate same-doctrine all-status realization sibling in
[document 170](170_p1a_all_status_transport_v2.md) combines only a freshly
reconstructed `STRONG` P1-A projection with an independently verified finite v1
state graph. Its complete `Ready|Blocked` commuting rows are bounded executable
evidence, not a generic naturality theorem, cross-doctrine conversion, vertical
cost law, observer admission, formation, or lifecycle implication.

Finite sample agreement must never be promoted to general refinement. The
translation must be derived from exact admitted structure or proved for the
declared carrier.

## 6. Positive constructibility

Constructibility asks whether an admitted process can produce this, not whether it exists absolutely.

The provisional P1-B result is only **formal finite generability**; its bounded judgment requires:

1. a fixed internal builder program;
2. admitted seeds and resource bounds;
3. fresh replay rather than inspection of the claimed target;
4. a canonical commitment to the replayed output;
5. exact agreement of that commitment with the target presentation.

Hashing a supplied stage proves only presentation commitment; callable/target-dependent builders, mutable sources, and foreign seeds cannot establish formal generability.
Even positive replay does not establish full constructibility, ontic genesis, chronology, or target-independent selection.

### 6.1 Scoped formation rule

The released finite scoped-formation judgment is ESTABLISHED only from an
independent bundle containing:

- replayed construction;
- exact doctrine admission;
- bound observer support where required by the declared scope;
- coherence evidence whose diagram nodes are bound to presentation stages;
- path persistence;
- confluence for every path/cycle demanded by that finite scope.
- survival under every genuine refinement demanded by that scope, or an
  explicit correct transport witness into the refined doctrine.

Missing evidence is OPEN. A bound obstruction may be REFUTED. The rule creates
neither primitive identity nor observer-free substance: it licenses only a
coherent formation in the named doctrine and finite diagram.

## 7. Confluence as the stabilization principle

Persistence answers whether one lineage carries a trace. Confluence answers
whether different allowed lineages can be reconciled.

For one finite fork, confluence requires a typed transport **2-cell** between
the two path histories, together with:

1. two distinct composable paths bound to the same presentation and fork;
2. a separate continuation from each endpoint;
3. one exact declared join stage;
4. compatibility of required observer responses/translations along both joined
   histories;
5. explicit reporting of the first split or missing witness.

The 2-cell witnesses that the two transported traces echo directly or after one
explicitly named response translation; a common endpoint identifier is not it.

P1 distinguishes:

- **fork confluence:** one declared pair of paths joins;
- **local finite confluence:** every declared one-step fork joins;
- **global finite confluence:** every required path/cycle in one finite diagram
  passes;
- **unbounded confluence:** not a P1 consequence.

Pairwise echo of endpoints is not a join. Reusing the same path twice is not a
fork. A standalone G4 atlas may demonstrate obstruction pressure, but it cannot
establish or refute a formation until atlas nodes and patch paths are bound to
the presentation.

## 8. From productivity to all depths

The central logical danger is a quantifier swap:

```text
for each finite depth, some extension was found
```

does not provide:

```text
one fixed generator that coherently produces every finite depth.
```

P1 calls a process **productive** only when one choice-free closed uniform rule
is supplied with a named basis for total/guarded next-step production and
coherent restriction. A
nonempty periodic prefix rule is a useful first structural example: the next
symbol and restriction law follow from its finite syntax rather than from a
long test run.

Even a structurally productive generator establishes only a pointwise schema:
every separately demanded finite stage can be answered by the same rule. P1
does not identify that schema with a supplied all-depth family or an extensional
completed totality.

An all-depth family therefore needs a separate status and provenance:

- **supplied hypothesis** — assumed as in I1's Lean tower;
- **formal derivation** — obtained from an accepted recursion/productivity proof;
- **oracle-dependent** — named but not internally grounded;
- **open** — only bounded probes or local witnesses exist.

No number of successful probes changes the provenance class.
## 9. PΩ — Separate completion principle
An all-depth compatible family still does not construct a carrier containing
its realization. Completion is doctrine-specific and must name:
1. a carrier presentation or constructor;
2. restriction observers at every admitted finite depth;
3. a realization obligation: restrictions reproduce the supplied family;
4. a relative-uniqueness obligation: two realizations echo under the declared
   complete restriction family;
5. the exact classical or native assumptions used.
This is a universal observer property, not representative equality. It permits
classical inverse limits, Cauchy completions, or coinductive carriers to be
studied as separate constructions without installing a global completion axiom
inside Veyra.
P1 defines only an OPEN interface/nonclaim boundary:
`COMPLETED_CARRIER=ESTABLISHED` is impossible in every P1 API and certificate.
Carrier, projections, realization, universal property, constructors, and any
nonemptiness/choice/compactness assumptions belong to a separate PΩ contract,
implementation, evidence tier, certificate, and review.

1. constant/blind process presented as an emerged observer;
2. post-target doctrine with a valid digest;
