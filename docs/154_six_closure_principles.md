# 154 — Six Closure Principles of Veyra

**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Status:** philosophical doctrine and implementation obligations; not a theorem  
**Scope:** exact, source-bound models; no absolute or physical ontology

## 1. Why six principles are needed

The existing kernel can describe distinctions, transitions, echoes, typed
silence, finite constructions, and several exact prime-power components. The
remaining gap is not one missing certificate. It is a nonlinear graph of six
different obligations that must not be collapsed:

```text
observer-role actualization       translation/refinement       path stability
productive continuation       all-depth family       completed carrier
                                  scoped object adoption
```

These are candidate typed edges, not a universal sequence: nodes may be
introduced independently, and adjacency promotes no status. Equal formulas,
matching digests, finite agreement, or successful QA create no edge.

The six contracts do not inhabit one layer. OAP/SCAP/RCPΩ contain
doctrine-admission steps; TTRP/PSP are semantic proof obligations; AAP requires
an accepted constructor or explicitly named foundation rule. Epistemic
provenance justifies exact judgments and QA can defeat an implementation claim,
but neither evidence nor QA elects a doctrine or proves an ontological
admission. A concrete result is established only relative to the exact
admission, semantic proof, provenance, and scope it actually consumes.

## 2. Principle O — observer actualization

Principle O is a family of two non-interchangeable admissions. `OEP` may admit
a scoped observer capability/role from exact genealogy, coupling domain,
discrimination, persistence, efficacy, and response algebra. `HAP` may
additionally actualize one historical token only when a first birth cut, closed
strict past, target independence, same-token future efficacy, and exact
counterfactuals are supplied.

```text
observer-role(scope) != historically-actualized-token(history,scope)
```

Call their non-collapsing family the **Observer Actualization Pattern** (`OAP`).
It is sufficient within its doctrines, not necessary for every conceivable
observer. E1/E4 are finite OEP/HAP shadows; the internal P3-N0 candidate proposes arithmetic `A-HAP` within its candidate doctrine.
The partial executable P3-OG slice supplies deterministic pool-selection
metadata, retained internal residue and a synthetic finite maintenance-control
comparison. A separate raw-cycle lane supplies authority-free least-first-return
pressure from `UNFORMED`. Historical blind/one-shot selection, typed history,
full formation/first closure, post-formation component ablation and a role
theorem remain required.

“Ontic” here means internal becoming, not an external tester being renamed:
the doctrine admits a birth rule, while an exact history must supply the first
event and later causal efficacy; the resulting token is absent from its own
ancestry. This is model-relative actuality, not physical or necessary being.

Self-observation is only a later scoped coupling whose input may encode an
earlier observer artifact. The new response/token/result may not occur in its
own source or strict-past ancestry. There is no final meta-observer: the new
coupling retains its own source, scope, and possible blindness. None of these
rules establishes consciousness, physical birth, or a view from nowhere.

## 3. Principle T — translation and genuine refinement

Two observers compare only through typed source/response maps with a declared
domain. A translation `(s_ab,t_ab)` is genuine only if it transports actual
sources/responses and satisfies the commuting law:

```text
t_ab(O_a(x)) ≈_b O_b(s_ab(x))
```
on every declared source in its domain. The formula abbreviates equality of
induced reachable `ObsResult` quotient classes. A refinement additionally preserves
the coarse response while retaining at least one distinction erased by it.
Identity, composition, domains, counterexamples, and path coherence are part
of the translation object; matching labels are not.

This is the **Typed Translation and Refinement Principle** (`TTRP`). It answers
how observers translate one another without making them identical. P3-T gives
one finite network; P3-N2 gives the exact thin prime-power reduction network.
N2 supplies observer-shaped projections/translations; its `rho` maps acquire
admitted observer roles/tokens only through a separate OEP/A-HAP instance such
as N0. Neither network proves a universal observer order, a coarse-to-fine
inverse, or completeness over all observers.

## 5. Principle S — stabilization across histories

Repeated residues belong to one path-stable formation only relative to an
explicit demanded path family. The **Path Stabilization Principle** (`PSP`)
establishes `path-stable-in(D,S,P)` from exact generated-path coverage and
transported reconciliation for the demanded paths `P`. At minimum it binds
source continuity (or a typed source bridge), preservation along each admitted
path, joinability of alternatives, and equality around every declared square.

Echo supplies local relative agreement; PSP explains why multiple demanded
echo histories reconcile. Confluence without transported equality is too weak,
and transported equality without a generated path universe is too weak. Joint
separation is an additional identity rule; refinement robustness is an
additional objectivity/adoption rule. A composite doctrine may require all
three, but PSP alone creates neither carrier equality nor objective-in status.

P3-C1, C2.2, N2, and future N4 cover different obligations. Genuine
higher-cell C2.3, generic Church–Rosser, and path-independent identity outside
the exact prime-power network remain `OPEN`.

## 6. Relative objectivity

For a claim/presentation `Q` over exact scope `S`, agreement becomes objective
only when it is forced by a declared observer family `J` and exact cover `U`:

- genuine translations and path demands commute on every overlap;
- local instances of `Q` satisfy the exact cocycle;
- `J` jointly separates candidates in the claimed identity;
- an explicit gluing rule constructs `Q_glued` uniquely and route-independently;
- every admitted refinement in `R` preserves the glued claim;
- omission/collusion attacks and remaining boundaries are recorded.

Call this `objective-in(Q_glued,D,S,J,U,identity,translations,path-demands,R)`.
“Coverage” never means completeness over every possible observer or refinement.
Counterpressure records attacks executed and boundaries still `OPEN`; it does
not rule out undeclared observers. A larger network may preserve the old result
or block/refute its proposed extension, but cannot retroactively falsify a
correctly scoped judgment. Observer-free truth requires a separate semantics
and is not produced by consensus alone.

## 7. Principle A — potential process to all-depth family

The process/family levels differ before any completed-quantifier reading:
```text
demand-wise: given requested n, some finite output can be sought
uniform-open: n:I |- eval(n):X(n)
intensional: one closed eval : Pi(n:I).X(n), with coherent restrictions
extensional: a separately adopted graph/domain whole for that term
```

The **All-depth Assembly Pattern** (`AAP`) is the umbrella name for the earlier
AFIP/UAIP boundary, not a new promotion rule. It has at least two distinct
lanes: constructor-backed family introduction, and correspondence to an
independently introduced family. Either lane must identify one coherent family;

this is an intensional all-input rule, not an extensionally completed graph or
index totality; neither lane supplies a selector or independent completion.

## 9. Ontology of silence

The following meanings are category-distinct, not globally mutually exclusive.
A concrete API may expose them as disjoint union arms only after fixing one
operation, layer, scope, and precedence.

| Judgment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RESPONSE_SILENT` | an admitted observer ran and returned its silent value |
| `INTRINSIC_SILENT` | the process is the native silence form |
| `OPERATIONALLY_ABSENT` | no event occurred in an exact finite window |
| `EXCLUDED_IN_D_S` | a positive proof excludes it in doctrine `D`, scope `S` |
| `BLIND_IN_O_S` | observer `O` cannot distinguish the candidates on `S` |
| `DOMAIN_BLOCKED` | the operation is undefined on this input |
| `OBSTRUCTED` | a named mathematical incompatibility is witnessed |
| `UNOBSERVED` | no admitted observation was performed |
| `EPISTEMICALLY_OPEN` | current evidence establishes neither side |
| `RESOURCE_REFUSAL` | evaluation stopped before semantic judgment |
| `UNRESOLVED_IN_D` | `D` has no deciding rule; formal independence is unproved |
| `DIVERGENT` | execution exceeds its declared termination contract |

`INCONSISTENT_D` is a separately certified doctrine-level meta-status, not a
response or silence constructor. Any inconsistency policy must name its
consequence relation/non-explosion rule and provenance; inconsistency itself
never supplies an ordinary positive judgment. Only positive exclusion yields

## 11. Independence and falsification

The six contracts are intentionally independent. OAP can actualize an
observer of a formation that SCAP never adopts. SCAP can adopt an
object before any self-observer exists. TTRP can compare observers while PSP
fails on a noncommuting path. AAP can introduce a family without RCPΩ forming a
carrier; RCPΩ can form a carrier before any particular productive process is
bridged into it. None is an alternative spelling of another.

Each positive instance must also expose its nearest decisive pressure:

| Principle | Decisive negative/OPEN pressure |
|---|---|
| OEP | failed genealogy/coupling/discrimination/persistence/efficacy/response algebra |
| HAP | circular past or failed birth/independence/counterfactual/future efficacy |
| TTRP | a declared response violates the commuting translation |
| SCAP | required support, persistence, confluence, or refinement survival fails |
| PSP | two demanded paths lack a join or their transported square disagrees |
| AAP | requested depths come from incompatible sources or no single family constructor exists |
| RCPΩ | only finite windows/all-depth families exist, without carrier realization and separation |

A counterexample refutes the exact scoped request, not the universal principle.
Missing evidence yields `OPEN`; operational failure yields its operational
status. QA must test all these branches, but a passing test only shows that the
implementation respects the doctrine on the tested artifact.
