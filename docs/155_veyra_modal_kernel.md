## 1. The missing philosophical move
Veyra cannot answer six different questions with one predicate called
`exists`. Its primitive philosophical claim is instead:
> A positive claim of being is an indexed modal commitment, never the mere
> success of an external test. Historical actuality is occurrence plus causal
> efficacy; mathematical objecthood is stabilization plus explicit adoption.
This does not reduce ontology to QA. A test may justify a judgment, but only an
explicit doctrine rule can admit a role, object or completed whole. Nor does it
make being purely conventional: after the rule is fixed, counterexamples,
failed transports and incompatible histories can refute an exact claim.
The kernel therefore uses **modal judgments**. Their indices are part of their
meaning:
```text
Formed[D](x)                  x is well-formed in doctrine D
Occurred[H,t](e)             event e belongs to history H before cut t
Realized[F](x,Q)             foundation F derives term x : Q
Adopted[D,S](x,k)            D adopts x under exact rule k and scope S
Observer[D,H,S](o)           o was formed, occurred and remains efficacious
Translates[S](A,B,T)         T transports actual A-responses to B-responses
Stable[D,S,P](x)             demanded paths P reconcile x in scope S
IntensionalRule[D,I,X,Eq](f) one closed coherent evaluator f : Πi:I.X(i)
CompletedWhole[D,F,U,S,k,q,c](X) D/F admits X under scope S and exact rule k
```
These are types of evidence, not Boolean labels. Dropping an index changes the
claim. No constructor for one judgment is silently a constructor for another.
## 2. Observer: from function to historical participant
An ordinary function is a typed rule from inputs to outputs. An observer is a
historical participant only when four independent debts are paid:
1. **pre-response formation:** a source-prior process closes into a persistent
   discriminating coupling before its decisive result exists;
2. **birth occurrence:** the formation event is a member of the committed
   history at a first cut and cannot be backdated by replay;
3. **causal efficacy:** matched interventions change later residues through the
   same token, rather than merely changing an external report;
4. **survival:** later allowed continuations preserve its lineage and response
   laws until an explicit death or obstruction event.
Thus:
```text
SourcePriorFormation[D,H,S](o,t0) + OccurredBirth[H,t0](o)
+ SameLineageEfficacy[D,H,S](o) + Survival[D,H,S](o)
  -> Observer[D,H,S](o)
```
The arrow is a named doctrine rule, not logical inevitability. Recognition
checks the debts after formation; it does not cause the birth. A replay creates
a new evidence event, not the historical event it describes.
Who observes the observer? Another already actualized token may receive an
encoding of the first observer through a typed translation. Self-observation is
the special case where a later state of the same token observes an earlier
state. No final observer is required. `PrimitiveHistoryBasis[D,H]` explicitly
commits the root, finite audit policy, source order and non-backdating law
without pretending an observer witnessed its own basis. Its absolute origin
remains outside this relative model; changing the basis changes the judgment.
## 3. Translation: objectivity without a view from nowhere
Two observers do not compare by matching labels or outputs. A translation binds
a pre-answer domain, source map, typed response-quotient map, equality laws and
totality on every reachable declared source, and must commute there:
```text
T_R(Obs_A(x)) = Obs_B(T_X(x)).
```
A refinement is either equivalence-like, with typed round trips/coherence, or
strict fine-to-coarse, with equality inclusion plus an exhibited separator.
Translations/refinements include identity and composition laws at matching
indices; strictness is never required of identity.
Objectivity is therefore **descent**, not majority agreement:
```text
local compatibility
+ exact overlap translations
+ atlas/path roots committed independently before q
+ jointly separating atlas
+ unique precommitted gluing
+ survival under every admitted refinement
  -> ObjectiveIn[D,S,Atlas](q).
```
This result is observer-relative but not arbitrary: after the atlas and laws
are fixed, one bad square, ambiguous gluing or refinement counterexample blocks
it. Enlarging the atlas asks a new judgment; it does not rewrite the old scope.
## 4. Existence is a profile, not a ladder of synonyms
Veyra separates at least these positive modalities:
| Modality | Positive content | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| `Presentable` | canonical description | consistency or construction |
| `Formed` | typed formation rules close | occurrence or adoption |
| `ConstructibleBy` | sealed constructor yields it | history or persistence |
| `Realized` | exact foundation derives it | operational occurrence |
| `Occurred` | event belongs to history | mathematical stability |
| `Persistent` | allowed continuations preserve it | refinement robustness |
| `Witnessed` | positive evidence was checked | objecthood |
| `Adopted` | doctrine performs a named admission | absoluteness |
| `ScopedObject` | construction, identity and adoption reconcile | completed infinity |
There is no universal total order among these states. Foundation realization
and historical occurrence are orthogonal. A formal term may never be executed;
an event may occur without denoting a stable mathematical object.
The positive object rule is consequently conjunctive:
```text
sealed construction or foundation realization
+ essential positive support
+ persistence over demanded continuations
+ reconciliation over demanded paths
+ robustness under admitted refinements
+ jointly separating presentation identity
+ usable doctrine and one source-prior formation index
+ explicit adoption
  -> ScopedObject[D,F,S,k](x).
```
Missing evidence gives `EPISTEMIC_OPEN`; a checked incompatible premise gives `SEMANTIC_REFUTED`;
runtime failure remains operational. Hash agreement and test coverage never
replace a conjunct.
This profile is not a bag of names: doctrine, scope, identity, source order,
rule, goal and capability kind remain inside judgment and evidence. Changing
one index asks another claim rather than decorating the old one.
### Silence is typed, not a negative answer
`ABSENT`, `OBSERVER_BLIND`, `OPERATION_UNDEFINED`, `OBSTRUCTED`, `UNKNOWN`,
`UNRESOLVED_IN_DOCTRINE`, `NO_ATTEMPT`, `NONRETURN` and `DIVERGED` are distinct
meanings with distinct evidence. Only checked absence can support a negative
phenomenon claim; blindness states a visibility limit, undefinedness a domain
failure, obstruction a blocked route, unknown an incomplete search, and
unresolvedness relative independence. Execution tags remain execution facts.
No tag coerces to another, to falsity, occurrence or nonexistence. The modal
status adapter preserves the tag and permits only an exact open-ledger or
relative-independence payload to enter the corresponding indexed status.
`ABSENT` here means **checked exclusion**, not missing evidence — the opposite
of `ABSENT`/OPEN in doc 150’s translation-evidence rule; the normative
disambiguation is `reference/silence-status-map.md` (registered via gap-audit
amendment A3, non-claim 7).
P3-M realizes this boundary as a literal nine-row dependent sum. Every silence
proof is indexed by the same header and exact arm goal; relative-independence
models additionally bind calculus, basis and the positive/negative goal through
a distinct sealed model-reference codec. Canonical decoding rejects model,
proof and cross-arm splicing.
## 5. One object across different histories
Echo is local agreement. Stable identity needs a category of demanded histories
or paths, fibers of presentations over them and typed transports. A candidate
formation is one object across those histories only when it forms a coherent
section:
```text
transport_id(s)=s
transport_(q∘p)(s)=transport_q(transport_p(s))
every declared square commutes
every admitted refinement has a restricting coherent lift.
```
Joinability alone does not identify endpoints. Equality of endpoints alone does
not identify traces. The path family and its closure rule must be committed
independently before the answer, or the claim becomes postselected.
This gives a model-relative conservation principle: the object is not an
unchanging hidden substance, but the equivalence class forced by coherent
transport and separation. Historical identity additionally requires the exact
events and cuts to occur; a formal coherent section is not retroactively a
history.
Generic confluence remains a separate theorem. Without termination or an
explicit higher-cell universe, local squares do not yield Church–Rosser,
unique normal forms or path-independent identity for all continuations.
## 6. Five meanings of “all finite depths”
The central infinity distinction is:
```text
P0 demand-wise:  requested n -> possibly some output at n
P1 uniform-open: n:I |- eval(n):X(n)
P2 intensional:  one closed coherent eval : Πn:I.X(n)
P3 extensional:  an independently constructed extensional family/graph
P4 completed:    a doctrine separately adopts a domain/carrier as a whole
```

P0 does not choose one family. P1 may still be open-context. P2 gives one
intensional term but does not construct its graph. P3 constructs an extensional
family without thereby adopting its domain or carrier. P4 is the new
foundational act. P3 and P4 are orthogonal: either requires its own constructor.

Hence productive continuation can reach an intensional all-input rule without
reaching actual infinity. König's lemma, dependent choice, compactness,
coinduction or a supplied inverse limit may bridge some settings, but each is a
source-bound theorem with an exact assumption ledger and independent extensional
constructor; renaming choice or an intensional evaluator is not such a bridge.
There is no generic cast from finite demands to an infinite whole.

## 7. Completed infinity is a separate adoption

Veyra treats completed infinity as a positive, foundation-relative modality:

```text
formed inhabited index/carrier
+ exact equality and order/projection laws
+ pre-adoption open-context escape or compatible-family structure
+ source-prior completion/adoption rule
+ checked application of that rule
  -> (CompletedWhole[D,F,U,S,k,q,c](X), OwnedAdoptionCapability[D,F,U,S,k,q,c,X]).
```

The owned capability is an output, never a premise of its own adoption. It is
decisive for later internalization. Formation, induction, successor, arbitrary
finite continuation, a no-greatest schema, an injection or the absence of
forbidden ancestry cannot invoke completed quantification. Only a successful
adoption rule returns the capability that internalizes the open schema as a
completed `forall`.

Only a separately rooted proof kernel may check the literal goal and premises;
a schema parser may only reject or prepare. Scoped-object, completed-index and
completed-carrier capabilities are mutually noncoercible. “No eliminator” means
no direct or unregistered eliminator: the interface names only indexed scoped
identity adoption and completed-index/carrier internalization, each preserving
doctrine, scope, formation, capability kind and target. Its four output rows
also carry the exact proof that the requested kind selects judgment-only,
scoped, index or carrier output; the interface cannot choose its own tag.

The current P3-M candidate has no admitted root capability and no positive trusted base. A future formalization must
bind all twenty-four manifest fields—exact source/import/declaration,
interface and certificate bytes, their equality/build claims and availability—
in one nonrecursive checked build tuple. Total bounded decoders must first bind
the complete `P3MAPP1` request to its rule, index, premises and goal statement,
then reconstruct the sealed application, handle, output and owned positive from
the same release. Goal statements and their single SHA-256 digest remain
different types, so neither a digest nor a second hash can impersonate a goal.

This is neither Platonist discovery from nowhere nor finitist denial. It is a
declared ontological commitment whose exact mathematical consequences can be
checked. Different foundations may accept, reject or translate it lossily.
Agreement becomes cross-foundation objectivity only through explicit
interpretations preserving formation, equality, quantifiers and adoption.

## 9. Forbidden generic inference rules

The modal kernel rejects every unowned rule of these forms:

```text
Observed(x)                         -/-> Occurred(x)
Formed(x)                           -/-> Adopted(x)
ConstructibleBy(C,x)                -/-> ScopedObject(x)
TestedCoherentToDepth(n,x)          -/-> StableOnAllPaths(x)
forall requested n, exists x_n      -/-> exists family f
IntensionalRule(f)                  -/-> CompletedGraph(f)
NoGreatestSchema(I)                 -/-> CompletedWhole(I)
Injection(Nat,C)                    -/-> Infinite(C) without CompletedNat
AtlasAgreement(q)                   -/-> absolute truth
Replay(e)                           -/-> OccurredInPast(e)
```

Positive rules are local constructors owned by exact doctrines. Their proof
dependencies form a partial DAG, not a fictitious universal chronology.

## 10. Honest remainder and next proof pressure

This calculus answers the six questions structurally, but does not close their
instances. The remaining obligations are concrete:

1. validate endogenous observer formation without importing its later answer;
2. prove real translation/refinement squares beyond finite chosen networks;
3. instantiate the full constructive-adoption conjunction without evidence
   splicing or postselection;
4. supply typed higher cells or termination where generic confluence is claimed;
5. distinguish and verify intensional all-input rules before any extensional
   family/whole claim;
6. review and instantiate an `INDEX_ONLY` completed-`Nat` adoption, then let
   N6C transport it through an independently proved injection.

The philosophical kernel is therefore sharper but deliberately incomplete.
Its progress is the removal of illicit implications: every future positive
claim now has a named ontological debt, semantic law, epistemic witness and QA
obligation, none of which may impersonate another.
