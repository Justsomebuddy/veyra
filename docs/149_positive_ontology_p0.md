# P0 — Positive Ontology and Ideological Nucleus
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Status:** provisional constitutive doctrine; not a theorem or scientific validation  
**Depends on:** manifesto, Core-0 vocabulary, R11 typed observer semantics, G4 global-obstruction warning, I1 infinity boundary
**Continuation:** `150_constructive_observer_doctrine_p1.md`; provisional P1 slices and exact-ledger PΩ1/PΩ2 are released, while productive-to-family passage and generic completion remain open
## 1. Why a positive nucleus is needed

Veyra began correctly by refusing several inherited defaults: primitive equality, points as location dust, segments as distance sticks, numbers as counts, and objects before relations. That refusal is an ideology of inquiry, not yet an ontology.

The project also developed a strong epistemic and engineering discipline: observer-indexed echo, explicit obstructions, bounded witnesses, certificates, formal bridges, and non-claim boundaries. Those practices say how claims are checked. They do not by themselves say what exists.

P0 therefore asks a narrower question:

> What is the smallest noncircular doctrine in which distinction, observation, persistence, and coherent presentation can be discussed without silently restoring primitive object identity?

The answer below is deliberately doctrine-relative and revisable. P0 does not prove a metaphysics.

## 2. Four levels that must not be mixed

| Level | Role | P0 examples | Not the same as |
|---|---|---|---|
| ideology | research commitments and refusals | anti-defaults, observer plurality, obstruction honesty | facts about reality |
| ontology | constitutive starting postulates | rez-event, coupling, continuation witness, persistent presentation | tests or theorem status |
| semantics/epistemology | meanings and justified judgments | observer response, echo, support, refutation, unknown | ontological generation |
| governance/QA | trust and release discipline | tests, certificates, fuzzing, coverage, reproducibility | ontological axioms |

The nine rows historically called “Essence axioms” are an executable policy contract spanning these levels. The eight F1 `AX-*` rows are operational kernel rules. Neither list alone is the positive ontology.

## 3. Metatheory boundary

Veyra removes equality as a primitive **object-language** relation. It does not pretend to eliminate syntactic identity, byte identity, or semantic equality from Python, Lean, or the surrounding metalanguage.

For a typed observer `o`, an internal echo judgment may be checked externally as:

```text
Echo_o(x, y; r)  when  run(o, x) = Ready(r)  and  run(o, y) = Ready(r).
```

The displayed `=` is metatheoretic bookkeeping. It is not internal Veyra identity. In particular:

```text
Echo_o(x, y) does not imply that the presentations x and y are metatheoretically identical.
```

No recursive attempt is made to replace the metalanguage’s equality with another echo; that would only move the regress.

## 4. Constitutive P0 postulates

These are labeled `P0-O*` rather than `AX-*` or `THM-*` because they are philosophical commitments, not executable axioms or proved theorems.

### P0-O1 — Co-constitutive rez

A `rez` is adopted as a primitive asymmetric event. It is not a comparison between two already individuated objects. Its occurrence co-constitutes distinguishable faces and leaves the possibility of a trace.

This chooses an **ontic** rather than purely operational foundation. An operational mismatch can witness a rez-response, but does not create the postulate by circular definition.

Open obligation: specify a richer native calculus of rez composition without converting faces into primitive substances.

### P0-O2 — Typed coupling

Observation is a coupling between a presentation and an observer process. A core observer is an internal, closed, typed Veyra process with a declared input domain and response kind. An arbitrary external callable may be a research shadow, but it has no automatic ontological authority.

The observer is not a detached “view from nowhere.” It is another process participating in a coupling.

P0 distinguishes three questions that must not be conflated: ontic genesis (how a coupling-capable process stabilizes), formal admission (why a closed typed program is allowed in `D`), and epistemic selection (why investigators chose this observer). P1-E1 now establishes one finite doctrine-relative role under explicit OEP; general/physical genesis, chronology, target independence, and consciousness remain open.

### P0-O3 — Independent observer admission

An observer is admitted only relative to a versioned doctrine `D`. Admission obligations—syntax, typing, domain, resource limits, permitted composition, refinement, and response translation—must be committed independently of the claim being tested.

An observer is not admissible merely because it preserves a desired law. This prevents the most direct observer-selection circularity.

Admission is a metatheoretic responsibility. P0 does not claim a final observer that certifies all observers.

Immutability is not chronology: a digest can prove that a doctrine did not change after commitment, but cannot by itself prove that it was chosen before its target was known. Target-independence therefore requires provenance—such as prior public registration or a target-blind protocol—in addition to structural validation. The executable P0 slice supplies one source-fixed pressure doctrine; it does not solve general observer admission.

### P0-O4 — Witness-bearing continuation

A transition from presentation `x` to presentation `y` is not persistence by itself. Persistence requires a composable continuation or transport witness naming what response or invariant is preserved, under which doctrine, and along which path.

Consequences:

- persistence is not token identity;
- reachability alone is insufficient;
- persistence may hold under `D₁` and fail under a refinement `D₂`;
- agreement of two paths requires a separate confluence or cycle-coherence result.

### P0-O5 — Coherent presentation, not primitive object

A first-stage “object” is a doctrine-relative coherent presentation: residues plus their continuation/translation witnesses over a declared diagram. Every required path and declared cycle must be checked. Pairwise agreement is insufficient, as the G4 triangle obstruction demonstrates.

P0 intentionally avoids claiming an observer-free substance or quotienting all echoing presentations into one identity-bearing object.

### P0-O6 — Scoped robustness

A claim is robust relative to `D` when it is preserved by the explicitly admitted refinement/translation family of `D`. This can justify a finite, doctrine-scoped form of intersubjective objectivity.

It does not justify “true for all conceivable observers.” A future independently admitted observer may split a present echo class.

### P0-O7 — Process-first infinity

Veyra grants ontological priority to finite presentations and productive continuation processes. A uniform generator with coherent finite restrictions may constitute an actual process with potentially unbounded continuation. It is not thereby a completed infinite totality.

A completed all-depth family or limit carrier requires a separately named completion principle and a construction or hypothesis at that stronger level.

## 5. Observer doctrine

A bounded doctrine should declare at least:

```text
D = (observer programs, domains, response kinds, resolutions,
     refinement maps, response translations, admissible transitions,
     resource and scope bounds).
```

### 5.1 Refinement

“Finer” has two possible components and must name which is claimed:

1. information refinement: a fine ready response determines the coarse response through a declared translation;
2. domain refinement: the fine observer is defined wherever the coarse observer is defined.

Information refinement without domain refinement is not strong refinement.

### 5.2 Observer translation

A translation between observers is a declared partial response transformation with an information-loss report. It may preserve a claim, reflect it, do both, or do neither. Translation failure and observer incomparability are first-class results.

### 5.3 Self-observation and regress

There is no requirement that every observer be validated by a privileged observer of the observer. An observer may become the encoded input of another typed process, but its admission rests on finite doctrine obligations. The doctrine remains revisable rather than grounded by an infinite certification tower.

This stops the operational regress; it does not settle every metaphysical question about consciousness or self-reference.

## 6. Existence is a profile, not a ladder

P0 rejects an unproved “existence lattice.” The following judgments are distinct and need not form a total order:

| Facet | Question |
|---|---|
| presented | is a finite representation currently supplied? |
| admissible | does it satisfy doctrine `D`? |
| observed | did an admitted observer return a ready response? |
| constructible | is there a declared process producing it? |
| coherent | do the required translation diagrams and cycles pass? |
| persistent | is there a composable preservation witness along the declared path? |
| witnessed | is positive support carried by replayable source evidence? |
| supported/refuted/open | what is the current evidence status of a claim? |
| object-status | is it accepted as a coherent presentation in the declared scope? |

Examples separating the facets:

- a supplied presentation can remain unobserved;
- an observer can return a silent response and still positively witness observation;
- an observation can be ready while persistence fails under refinement;
- local next-step witnesses can exist while a global cycle is obstructed;
- a blocked observation neither proves absence nor refutes existence.

The bounded executable P0 slice therefore uses separate typed judgments and only `supported / open` existence evidence. It deliberately has no `nonexistent` result.

## 7. Persistence and coherent formation

For a finite path `h : x₀ → x₁ → … → xₙ`, write informally:

```text
Persist_D(h, w)
```

only when `w` contains composable step witnesses and the declared inherited observers preserve their named responses along the path.

Robustness under a stronger observer family is checked separately. Thus a formation may persist under a coarse crest observer while a newly admitted tail observer splits it. This is not a contradiction: persistence and robustness answer different scoped questions.

Path independence, transitivity outside explicitly composable witnesses, and all-cycle coherence are theorem obligations, not consequences of the word “persistence.”

This makes persistence genealogical rather than substantial: a formation continues because an admitted witness transports the declared trace, not because an invisible substrate stays numerically identical. If a lineage branches and no confluence witness reconciles the branches, P0 keeps two unresolved continuations; it does not decree one object by name alone.

## 8. From relative echo to scoped objectivity

P0 distinguishes:

1. **local echo:** one observer returns the same ready response;
2. **doctrine agreement:** every currently declared observer in `D` agrees where defined;
3. **refinement robustness:** the claim survives a fixed admitted refinement family;
4. **translated agreement:** responses commute through declared observer translations;
5. **conflict:** an admitted observer supplies a mismatch or incompatible translation;
6. **incomparability:** both scoped relation directions have distinct crossing
   refutations; absent translation/common domain remains OPEN, not incomparable.

Only levels 3–4 support the provisional phrase “doctrine-scoped objectivity.” The executable P0 slice currently reaches only fixed-family-extension pressure, not a genuine refinement or translation result. Absolute, observer-free truth is not claimed.

A mathematical law must also name its carrier, hypotheses, doctrine, and quantifier scope. A bounded certificate remains a finite regularity, not a universal law.

## 9. Typed silence

The following modes must never collapse into one Boolean failure:

| Mode | Meaning |
|---|---|
| response-silent | the observer ran and returned the silent mark |
| intrinsic-silent | the presented process is the native silence form |
| domain-blocked | the observer cannot apply on the presented domain |
| unobserved | no observation was requested or admitted |
| epistemically-open | neither support nor refutation is currently available |
| resource-limited | evaluation stopped at a declared finite bound |
| divergent | the process did not terminate within its contract |
| operationally-absent | no event was presented in a declared finite window |
| inconsistent | incompatible evidence is present |
| unresolved-in-system | a fixed formal system supplies neither a proof nor a refutation, when established at that level |

Response silence may be positive evidence. Domain blockage is not response silence. “Absent” and “unresolved-in-system” require evidence stronger than failure to observe.

## 10. Infinity boundary exposed by I1

P0 separates five claims:

1. a bounded window through depth `N` was checked;
2. one presented stage has an explicit next-stage witness;
3. a declared productive procedure returns a next stage;
4. an all-depth coherent family is supplied or constructed;
5. a completed carrier of compatible families is constructed.

Successful finite checks prove only claim 1. Repeated local extension witnesses do not automatically supply one coherent all-depth family. I1’s Lean tower assumes claim 4 as a hypothesis; it does not derive it from the bounded Python window. Claim 5 requires a separate completion construction.

The executable P0 report labels one-step evidence as `local-extension` and keeps productive processes, all-depth families, and completed carriers as explicit unverified boundaries, never finite promotions.

## 11. Mandatory counterexample pressure

Before P0 can become more than a provisional doctrine, executable rows must exhibit:

1. a constant observer collapsing distinct presentations;
2. a fixed observer-family extension splitting a former echo, followed later by a genuine declared refinement/translation counterexample;
3. ready silent response versus blocked observation;
4. pairwise-compatible but globally obstructed persistence;
5. two continuation paths without established confluence;
6. locally extendable stages without a supplied global tower;
7. incomparable observer judgments;
8. a false “law” manufactured by choosing observers after seeing the target.

These cases do not refute P0. They define the pressure its words must survive.

## 12. Ideological commitments

P0 commits Veyra to:

- no view from nowhere;
- no arbitrary observer dictatorship;
- no conversion of ignorance into absence;
- no conversion of finite success into completed infinity;
- no conversion of echo into hidden object identity;
- no conversion of tests or certificates into facts about reality;
- explicit scope, explicit losses, explicit obstructions, and revisable doctrines.

The aim is not to replace one dogma with alien vocabulary. It is to make the assumptions that produce objects, laws, and infinities visible enough to challenge.

## 13. Current non-claims

P0 does not establish:

- that rez-events are physical constituents of reality;
- a complete theory of observer genesis or self-reference;
- absolute objectivity;
- a general quotient or inverse-limit carrier;
- that coherent presentations are ontologically novel relative to classical diagrams;
- a theorem-derived Essence layer or an R8 promotion;
- a unified native numberhood criterion for modes, balances, ratios, and p-adic shadows.

The last item remains a separate P0/N3 question: a future Veyra number should be an echo-stable recurrent or completion carrier whose operations commute with declared observer restrictions, with explicit bridges for each realization.
