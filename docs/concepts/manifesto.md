# Manifesto: Do Not Start Where Humans Usually Start

## Goal

Create a mathematical system that is alien enough to escape inherited habits, but disciplined enough to produce proofs, models, counterexamples, and eventually scientific tools.

## Scope boundary

This manifesto states Veyra's **ideology of inquiry**: motivations, refusals,
and revisable research commitments. It is not a list of facts about reality.
The canonical provisional positive ontology is `../concepts/positive_ontology_p0.md`;
`../concepts/primitives.md` gives its semantic vocabulary, while proof and release QA
remain in `../log/proof_discipline.md` and `../concepts/foundational_gap_audit.md`.
The strictly reviewed `../concepts/constructive_observer_doctrine_p1.md` has provisional
P1-A structural and P1-B formal-generability lanes, not metaphysical results.
P1-D2 now blocks five finite-to-universal inferences with two insufficiency
judgments and three countermodels, without proving nonexistence; formation,
all-depth introduction, and PΩ completion remain separate/open.

## Anti-defaults

1. **No primitive equality.**  
   Use echo-equivalence: two constructions are the same only relative to tests that cannot distinguish them.

2. **No primitive point as location.**  
   A point-like thing is a residue of a distinction, not a coordinate.

3. **No primitive segment as distance.**  
   A segment-like thing is a directed breathing/tethering process, not a length.

4. **No primitive number as count.**  
   A number-like thing is a closed recurrence mode.

5. **No claim that objects come before relations.**  
   In Veyra, stable objects are leftovers of repeated distinction.

## Guiding image

Reality is not assumed to be a box filled with things. It is treated as a self-refining field of distinctions. Stable structures are residues that survive repeated cuts; laws are invariants of possible transformations; number is recurrence; geometry is tethered availability.

## First constraint

The first layer must be simple enough to test against ordinary natural numbers, points, and line segments. If it cannot rebuild the simplest structures, it is not yet useful.
