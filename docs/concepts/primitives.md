This file gives the **semantic vocabulary** of the Core-0 seed. The provisional
ontological commitments that motivate it live canonically in

## 1. Primitive vocabulary

### Rez

A **rez** is an act of distinction. It is not a set split; it is the event of making two faces no longer interchangeable.

Status: primitive intuition, not yet formalized as an object.

### Nod

A **nod** is the stable residue/boundary left by a rez.

Human shadow: a point.  
Veyra meaning: not location, but a minimal stable address of distinguishability.

### Tact

A **tact** is the smallest registered transition between nods.

Human shadow: unit step, edge, tick.  
Veyra meaning: the minimal detectable change of residue-facing.

### Breath

A **breath** is a directed finite tether of tacts from one nod to another.

Human shadow: segment/path.  
Veyra meaning: transfer pattern between residues.

### Mode

A **mode** is a closed breath: a recurrence that returns to its own boundary.

Human shadow: natural number when there is one tact kind.  
Veyra meaning: stable recurrence form.

### Echo-equivalence

Two constructions are **echo-equivalent** if all currently admitted tests return the same responses on them.

Human shadow: equality/isomorphism/congruence depending on context.  
Veyra meaning: indistinguishability by a specified test family.

## 2. Minimal axioms: Core-0

The `AX-001..007` labels below are the historical Core-0 seed. They are not the
P0 ontological postulates and are not the current executable F1 `AX-*` registry;
the operational source of truth is `reference/axioms.md`.

**AX-001 — Residue.** A completed rez leaves at least one nod.

**AX-002 — Directed tether.** A breath has a start-nod and an end-nod.

**AX-003 — Empty breath.** For every nod there is a silent breath from that nod to itself.

**AX-004 — Stitch.** If breath `x` ends where breath `y` starts, there is a stitched breath `x ⊙ y`.

**AX-005 — Echo associativity.** `(x ⊙ y) ⊙ z` and `x ⊙ (y ⊙ z)` are echo-equivalent when both are defined.

**AX-006 — Closure.** A breath whose end echoes its start can be closed into a mode.

**AX-007 — One-tact seed.** The first arithmetic layer admits one distinguished tact `τ`.

## 3. Human-shadow model for consistency

To keep the seed honest, Core-0 has a standard shadow:

- nods shadow vertices;
- tacts shadow directed edges;
- breaths shadow finite directed paths;
- empty breath shadows the empty path;
- stitch shadows path concatenation;
- modes shadow closed paths;
- in the one-nod one-tact layer, modes shadow words `τ^n`, hence natural numbers `n`.

This model is not the final philosophy; it is the sanity check.
