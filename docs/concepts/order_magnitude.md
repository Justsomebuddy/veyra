# Dominance Order and Magnitude

## 1. Purpose

School mathematics needs comparison: greater, less, equal, intervals, and absolute value.

Veyra treats comparison as **dominance under an observer**, not as a universal fact about objects.

## 2. Balance dominance

**DEF-051 — Length dominance.**

For balances `A` and `B`, define:

`A ≻_len B` iff `len±(A ⊟ B) > 0`.

The comparison result carries a gap balance, not just a sign.

## 3. Magnitude

**DEF-052 — Balance magnitude.**

The length magnitude of a balance is:

`|B|_len = τ^{abs(len±(B))}`.

This is not an internal universal norm; it is a one-tact shadow of unresolved opposition.

## 4. Ratio dominance

**DEF-053 — Ratio dominance.**

For ratio modes `Q` and `R`:

`Q ≻_rat R` iff `shadow(Q-R) > 0`.

This recovers rational inequalities in the length-shadow layer.

## 5. Ratio interval

**DEF-054 — Ratio interval.**

A ratio interval is a pair of ratio bounds plus open/closed endpoint flags.

Membership is observer-relative:

`Q ∈ [L,U]` iff `shadow(L) ≤ shadow(Q) ≤ shadow(U)`.
