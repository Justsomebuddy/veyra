# Gap to a Full School-Replacement Mathematics

## 1. Current status

Veyra Core 1.5.1 can model finite modes, echo tests, resonance, defects, weighted defects, derived tact similarity, spectra, compression, and counterexamples.

This is a living seed, not yet a full mathematical civilization.

## 2. Missing replacement layers

To replace the school apparatus up to grade 11, Veyra needs complete analogues for:

1. **Arithmetic:** negative modes, fractions, ratios, powers, roots, divisibility towers.
2. **Algebra:** equations as resonance constraints, transformations, identities, factorization, polynomials.
3. **Order:** comparison, magnitude, intervals, monotonicity, inequalities.
4. **Geometry:** surface, angle, area, volume, curvature, coordinate shadows.
5. **Functions:** mode-transformers, composition, inverse, graph analogues, growth.
6. **Analysis seed:** limit, continuity, derivative, integral as resonance-flow concepts.
7. **Probability/statistics:** unstable modes, distributions over resonance states, expectation analogue.
8. **Logic/proof:** formal inference rules, theorem language, proof checker target.
9. **Measurement:** units, dimensional analysis, approximation, error propagation.
10. **Pedagogy:** ladder from child intuition to formal Veyra syntax.

## 3. Deep capacity requirement

Each traditional concept must not merely be renamed. It must be compressed into a deeper Veyra-native primitive:

- equality → observer-indexed echo;
- number → recurrence mode;
- divisibility → resonance;
- similarity → shared aura;
- error → structured defect;
- formula → reusable resonance schema;
- proof → obstruction-eliminating path.

## 4. Optimization requirement

The system must become smaller than school math in primitives, but larger in expressive reach.

Target:

- fewer primitives;
- more derived concepts;
- every concept executable in finite shadows;
- every theorem linked to counterexample pressure;
- every operation declared with its echo-compatibility scale.
