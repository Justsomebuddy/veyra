## Research discipline
Every beautiful idea must be marked as one of:

- definition,
- axiom,
- lemma/theorem,
- conjecture,
- heuristic,
- analogy,
- aesthetic signal.

No metaphysical claim is accepted as mathematics until it becomes a definition, theorem, model, counterexample, or reproducible computation.
