# Foundational Gap Audit — no self-deception gate

**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** critical audit; blocks grand claims until repaired.  
**Source:** direct project critique after Sprint X5.  
**Scope:** axioms, theorem language, formal proof, native runtime, number theory, and classical comparison.

## Current hard truth

Veyra currently demonstrates **expressive workability** across finite shadows and certificate fixtures. It does **not** yet demonstrate a unified alternative mathematics.

The active repository has many executable layers, but most are isolated islands. They are certified by tests on bounded fixtures, not by a shared deductive foundation.

## Non-claims now mandatory

The project must not claim:

1. that Veyra is a complete mathematics;
2. that general Core/VAM semantics are covered by the R10/R11 closed recurrence and observer slices;
3. that Python certificates are formal proofs unless they pass their exact R7–R11 artifact, contract, carrier, elaboration, observer, and Lean bindings;
4. that topology/geometry/algebra are derived from arithmetic or from one axiom base;
5. that native Veyra ontology is complete beyond the bounded F4 runtime.
6. that the provisional P0 doctrine proves physical/metaphysical reality,
   absolute objectivity, or existence of a completed infinite carrier.

`149_positive_ontology_p0.md` is the canonical P0 statement. Its ideological,
ontological, semantic/epistemic, and governance levels must remain distinct;
the operational F1 axioms and nine mixed Essence policies are not substitutes
for that separation.
`150_constructive_observer_doctrine_p1.md` continues that doctrine without
promotion: finite P1-A2/P3-T relations are not universal observer order;
released E1/E4 roles/tokens are not physical genesis or consciousness;
released C2/C3/C4 and exact-ledger PΩ1/PΩ2 remain independent scoped lanes.
Arithmetic N3/N4 realization/equality are released; N0/N5 and genuine
higher-cell C2.3 are still unreleased/open.
