# P3-OG — Endogenous Arithmetic Observer Formation
**Document kind:** public research specification, not a theorem or release  
**Target:** one finite, source-bound arithmetic model  
**Status:** partial executable pressure slices; every positive theorem remains
`OPEN` until formalized
## 1. The idea in child-sized language
An ordinary test is like a ruler placed beside a thing. The ruler does not become
an observer merely because we read a number from it.
P3-OG asks for something stronger:
1. choose one possible little process before knowing which answer would help;
2. let that process form and keep an inside boundary of its own;
3. let two arithmetic encounters leave two different traces inside it;
4. show that the trace is still there later;
5. show that the retained trace changes what the same process later does;
6. remove the relevant inside part and show that this ability disappears.
Recognition comes last. The checker may confirm that formation occurred, but it
must not choose the successful process after seeing the result or manufacture the
event by naming it.
## 2. Exact claim boundary
P3-OG may establish only:
```text
EndogenousArithmeticObserverRole[D,H,S](o)
```
for one doctrine `D`, finite history `H`, coupling scope `S`, and process token
`o`. It does not by itself establish:
```text
HistoricallyActualized[D,H,S](o)     -- requires a separate HAP application
PhysicalObserver(o)
Conscious(o)
AbsoluteObserver(o)
ScopedObject(o)
CompletedInfinity(...)
```
Arithmetic N0/A-HAP and P3-OG are not interchangeable. The internal N0
candidate is designed to authenticate a history-relative token for a
predeclared, stateless `rho_n` role. P3-OG must add
blind one-shot selection, stateful retained residue, endogenous boundary
maintenance, and removal/ablation dependence. If a future P3-OG role theorem is
established, its role witness may be supplied to a separately premised HAP
application; it is not already a HAP token.

### 2.1 Current executable slice

`prime_power_observer_genesis_p3og*` currently implements only a falsifiable
finite machine experiment:

- the canonical raw seed pool, source-instance label, arithmetic scope,
  caller-supplied bounded signed-integer calibration inputs, maintenance credit
  and suffix are source-committed;
- a nonce-free deterministic selector records metadata from the rule, arithmetic
  scope, doctrine label and pool; it is not a historical blind or consumed
  one-shot capability and it does not determine the all-candidate verdict;
- every canonical candidate is checked, and any failed candidate refutes only
  this conservative pool-robustness pressure experiment;
- the machine starts with an `ALIVE` boundary; a recurrent seed shape is used as
  a response table, not replayed as a formation genealogy or first closure;
- a retained finite residue affects a later `ADVANCE` phase, while native
  maintenance-credit reset/decay is compared with a synthetic pre-coupling
  maintenance-flag control under the same externally driven suffix;
- before suffix execution, both calibration inputs must produce the same
  response and the same semantic post-coupling state on the active and disabled
  arms. The semantic projection is derived from the state schema and excludes
  only `maintenance_control` and `state_digest`, so future semantic fields are
  compared by default. Expected flags and exact input, state-link, and digest-
  bound receipts are also required; the accepted precomputed coupling evidence
  feeds the suffix trace directly. Any mismatch refutes the candidate as
  `matched-control-coupling-drift` before downstream discrimination;
- exact bounded replay validation rejects structural/source/result drift, keeps
  role status `OPEN`, and keeps promotions at zero; the accepted 64-candidate by
  64-step envelope executes under separate fixed source and evidence budgets;
- exact source tuple cardinality and string character bounds are preflighted
  before nested traversal or UTF-8 encoding, canonical integers are limited to
  4096 bits, and a removed boundary is terminal at every public low-level
  operation. Internal complete-suffix evidence retains later terminal steps only
  as distinct budget-consuming removed-state no-ops.

A separate `prime_power_observer_genesis_p3og_lifecycle` facade adds one
authority-free raw-cycle first-return pressure lane without changing those
machine/report bytes:

- it starts an independent linear pressure state at `UNFORMED` and accepts no
  caller-supplied symbol, closure index, target, result, or status;
- each native tick consumes only the next value of the exact committed seed
  cycle and binds a continuous before/after receipt chain;
- it stops at the least return to the seed mode after a genuine departure;
- its lifecycle source is the exact word fixed by the existing deterministic
  selected-seed receipt, not a caller-chosen candidate or closure target;
- a passing endpoint binds to the existing operational `ALIVE` pressure-entry
  state digest, but the binding is fresh checker replay, not historical
  authentication, event creation, observer authority, or a theorem that the
  raw-cycle return predicate is invariant under operational machine semantics.

A third, explicitly non-root
`prime_power_observer_genesis_p3og_formation_pressure` facade now supplies the
first typed consumer relation between those two independently replayable lanes.
Its producer freshly validates the exact pressure source, formation source,
`WITNESSED` first-closure evidence and complete pressure report. It then requires
one identical deterministic selection receipt and selected seed, reconstructs
the operational entry state, and binds that state to both the lifecycle handoff
and the selected pressure candidate's active pre-coupling evidence. The selected
candidate result and its exact `PASSED` or `REFUTED` status are retained without
promotion. A later pressure refutation therefore does not invalidate the
identity relation, while a lifecycle `REFUTED` result cannot produce a positive
binding.

```text
WITNESSED first-closure evidence
  + exact same-source selected pressure replay
  -> non-promoting formation-pressure binding
```

The bridge digest has its own versioned domain and is reconstructed from replayed
identities, not caller-supplied authority. It does not turn formation evidence
into an observer-role judgment and it creates no doctrine admission, typed
history, HAP witness, birth token, ablation result, causal-efficacy judgment,
theorem, source authentication or truth claim.

The terminal seed coordinate exposes the distinction. The low-level
operational machine reads `cycle[:-1]`: its response lookup and `ADVANCE`
period are unchanged between `(0,1,0)` and `(0,1,2)`, and `MAINTAIN`/`IDLE`
do not inspect cycle values. Across the fixed bounded input/schedule regression,
those words therefore have the same response and semantic state evolution once
source/seed identity digests are excluded. The lifecycle lane intentionally
reads the full committed word: the first is `WITNESSED` at index 2 and the
second is `REFUTED`. Thus `pressure_entry_state_digest` is an exact identity and
replay link for witnessed evidence, not an operational-representation-
invariance result. Any future semantic formation layer must define and validate
its own native transition-state closure contract rather than reinterpret this
raw-cycle v1 evidence.

This establishes only a bounded linear first-return **pressure analogue** and an
exact relation to the selected operational execution. The operational pressure
machine still begins `ALIVE`; neither the lifecycle lane nor its consumer is a
typed history `H`, source-ancestry proof, historical chronology, primitive Veyra
genealogy, or the full `FirstClosure(M,H,t0,q0)` predicate below.

These slices do **not** yet supply a typed history DAG, a full historical
first-closure proof, a historical one-shot selector, a typed post-formation
component ablation, an exact N1/N2/P3-T input bridge, a Lean theorem, an N0/HAP
lift, an observer-role judgment, or the full DEF-OG-001..010 conjunction. The
lifecycle status concerns only the exact selected committed word; the binding
only relates it to the exact selected execution; separately, a positive
machine-pressure report still means that every candidate in one committed finite
pool passed its implemented checks.

## 3. Finite formal universe
Fix:

I       : finite arithmetic input type

The concrete arithmetic inputs must be freshly derived from the exact prime-power

## 4. Proposed core definitions
### DEF-OG-001 — Stateful candidate
A candidate is a closed finite transition system
```text
M = (Q, q_seed, tick, couple, read, residue, boundary, alive)
tick   : Q -> Q
couple : Q x I -> Q
read   : Q -> Y
residue: Q -> R
boundary : Q -> B
alive  : Q -> Bool
```
Its transition code, types, resource bounds, and source identity are committed
before selection and before any decisive response. A stateless map `I -> Y`
cannot inhabit this definition.
### DEF-OG-002 — Blind one-shot selection
Let `Pool` be a nonempty, precommitted finite tuple of candidate sources.
```text
select : Pool x BlindSeed -> CandidateId
```
`BlindSelection(Pool, seed, c, H)` holds only if:
1. `Pool`, `seed`, and `select` occur in the strict past;
2. the discrimination criterion, target, selected responses, later status, and
   theorem conclusion are absent from their transitive source closure;
3. `c = select(Pool, seed)` is evaluated exactly once;
4. the selection capability is consumed and cannot be retried;
5. failures remain failures; there is no choose-until-success loop.
Blindness is a dependency property, not merely a hash or a claim that the
investigator did not look.
### DEF-OG-003 — Internal formation and first closure
`FirstClosure(M,H,t0,q0)` holds when `q0` is reached by a nonempty, replayable
`tick` genealogy from `q_seed`, `alive(q0)=true`, the required boundary is present,
no earlier state in that lineage satisfies the same closure predicate, and the
decisive criterion/result is not an ancestor of `t0`.
The closure predicate must inspect native state and transition structure. An
external label `formed=true` or a checker-created token is insufficient.
### DEF-OG-004 — Retained discriminating residue
For two allowed inputs `a != b`, starting from the same closed state `q0`, let
```text
qa = couple(q0,a)
qb = couple(q0,b).
```
`RetainsDifference(M,q0,a,b,k,c)` holds when:
1. `residue(qa) != residue(qb)`;
2. `k >= 1`;
3. applying the same precommitted continuation `c` for `k` transitions preserves
   distinct internal residues;
4. the distinction is in `Q/R`, not only in an external log or digest.
### DEF-OG-005 — Endogenous boundary maintenance and removal
`MaintainsBoundary(M,q0,C)` holds when every continuation in the declared finite
catalog `C` is executed by `M` itself and preserves a well-typed live boundary.
`InternallyRemovesBoundary(M,q,u)` holds only when a declared native transition,
driven by state/internal signal `u`, removes that boundary without a fixture or
checker rewriting `Q`. Witnesses must identify the maintaining component and the
native removal transition. An external reset or checker repair refutes this debt.
### DEF-OG-006 — Typed internal ablation
`ablate : ComponentId x Q -> Q` is admitted only as a matched intervention that:
1. removes or disables one predeclared internal maintenance component;
2. leaves code identity, arithmetic input, external schedule, scope, and all
   unrelated state equal;
3. cannot directly overwrite the claimed later response;
4. is applied after formation and before the later efficacy observation.
### DEF-OG-007 — Removal dependence
For a maintenance component `u`, `RemovalDependent(M,u,q,C)` holds when the
unablated run maintains the boundary over `C`, while the matched `ablate(u,q)`
run loses that boundary, loses the retained distinction, or reaches a named
internal obstruction. Merely changing an output label does not count.
### DEF-OG-008 — Same-token causal efficacy
`CausallyEffective(M,o,qa,qb,c,j)` holds when two matched histories:
1. share candidate, birth/closure, token `o`, lineage, scope, code, and future
   continuation `c`;
2. differ only in the earlier allowed coupling or the typed ablation;
3. remain distinct after at least one later internal transition;
4. produce a different later transition, readiness state, or typed response at
   observation cut `j`;
5. bind that difference to the retained residue or removed component through the
   declared transition law.
Correlation of two logs without a matched intervention is not causal efficacy.
### DEF-OG-009 — Noncircular history
`NoncircularOG(H,o,t0)` requires:
- typed, acyclic parent edges and graph-derived ancestry;
- exact source closure for every event;
- no target, decisive result, theorem conclusion, later status, oracle answer,
  HAP/N0 token, or `o` in the strict past of `t0`;
- `o` absent from its own ancestry and proof union;
- doctrine, pool, selector, scope, criterion, continuation catalog, ablation
  component, and comparison cuts committed before their results;
- post-closure evidence used only forward, never to authenticate formation.
### DEF-OG-010 — Endogenous arithmetic observer role
`EndogenousArithmeticObserverRole[D,H,S](o)` is admitted only by the named P3-OG
rule from independent witnesses of DEF-OG-001 through DEF-OG-009 plus a typed
response algebra and exact doctrine admission. It is a role judgment, not a
definition of physical existence.
## 5. Theorem obligations
### THM-P3OG-001 — Concrete arithmetic formation
Construct one explicit finite candidate pool and prove that the one-shot rule
classifies every possible selection without retry. For a precommitted positive
selection, prove:
1. the selected candidate reaches a first internal closure;
2. fresh arithmetic inputs derived from `F_0/F_1` leave distinct retained
   residues;
3. one nontrivial common continuation preserves the distinction;
4. the retained residue changes a later transition/response;
5. the candidate internally maintains its boundary over the declared catalog;
6. ablating the named maintenance component destroys the exact claimed ability;
7. the complete event/source DAG is noncircular.
Positive conclusion:
```text
exists o, EndogenousArithmeticObserverRole[D,H,S](o).
```
The proof must construct the candidate and histories; a theorem taking all seven
conclusions as opaque premises is only an interface lemma.
### THM-P3OG-002 — Recognition does not create formation
Replaying the checker against an unchanged committed history preserves the role
judgment but creates only a new evidence event. It does not create or backdate
`t0`, the selected candidate, its state, or token `o`.
### THM-P3OG-003 — Ablation separator
For the concrete model, prove that unablated and correctly ablated matched runs
cannot have both the same maintained boundary and the same claimed later efficacy.
### OBL-P3OG-004 — HAP type boundary
Expose only an explicitly premised lift:
```text
hapLift : EndogenousArithmeticObserverRole[D,H,S](o)
       -> HAPWitness[D,H,S](o)
       -> HistoricallyActualized[D,H,S](o).
```
A role-only eliminator is forbidden. This interface absence is not itself a
logical non-implication theorem; such a theorem would require a countermodel.
`HAPWitness` separately supplies first birth, strict past, target independence,
same-token future, and historical occurrence.
## 6. Three-way semantic status
For each requested role judgment:
```text
ESTABLISHED  all exact obligations hold in the named finite scope
REFUTED      a typed witness contradicts a required obligation
OPEN         required evidence or a bridge is absent, but no contradiction is proved
```
Operational results remain separate:
```text
RESOURCE_REFUSAL | COMPILE_FAILURE | CONTINUITY_DRIFT | DIVERGENT
```
They never coerce to `OPEN`, `REFUTED`, absence, or observer nonexistence.

## 8. Mandatory nonclaims
No P3-OG result may claim necessity of this observer criterion, universal
observer genesis, arbitrary refinement, observer-independent identity,
consciousness, biological or physical emergence, free will, absolute causation,
metaphysical being, object adoption, p-adic carrier formation, completed infinity,
operational representation invariance for raw-cycle lifecycle evidence, or
promotion of a formation-pressure identity binding into a role, HAP witness,
token, truth claim, or combined N0/HAP/P3-OG theorem.
## 9. Release threshold
Before any public theorem status:
1. definitions and exact theorem statements exist in Lean or another declared
   proof foundation;
2. the concrete state machine is constructed rather than postulated;
3. theorem dependencies exclude result/target/HAP/N0 circularity;
4. all matrix rows have focused positive/adversarial tests;
5. source continuity, theorem registry, notation, nonclaims, and certificate
   agree byte-for-byte;
6. public status says exactly `FORMALLY_PROVED`, `PUBLICLY_VALIDATED`, or `OPEN`
   according to the evidence actually present.
Until then this file is a design debt ledger, not evidence that an observer has
formed.
