# Comparative Bridge and Separation Ledger

**Status:** bounded executable/Lean-backed truth-maintenance slice

**Source:** public design discussion in issue #5

**Scope:** structural comparisons only; the fixed eight-row F5 result ledger is unchanged

## Why this ledger is separate

F5 compares named results and artifacts with local verdicts such as `weaker`,
`clearer`, or `stronger`. A structural bridge instead asks whether a Veyra
construction is an analogue of, reduces to, or remains open against another
formalism. A structural separation asks whether two explicit predicates differ.
Mixing these would let analogy masquerade as a theorem or a predicate
counterexample masquerade as superiority.

The implementation therefore has disjoint status spaces:

- bridge: `KNOWN_ANALOGUE`, `CANDIDATE_BRIDGE`, `REDUCED`, `OPEN`;
- separation: `CANDIDATE_SEPARATION`, `STRICTLY_SEPARATED`, `OPEN`.

`REDUCED` requires a stated correspondence plus checked evidence.
`STRICTLY_SEPARATED` requires an explicit checked theorem or counterexample.
Neither status implies novelty, nonexpressibility, or global superiority.

## Initial bridge rows

| ID | Veyra construct | Comparison | Status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CB-ECHO-001` | observer-indexed echo | indexed binary relations | `KNOWN_ANALOGUE` | vocabulary-level analogy only |
| `CB-PROCESS-001` | finite observer process semantics | coalgebra/bisimulation | `OPEN` | no functor, preservation, or reflection theorem |
| `CB-G4-001` | finite G4 exact gluing existence | matching-family amalgamation for `V ↦ EqRel(V)` | `REDUCED` | one declared finite cover; existence/effectivity only |

For `CB-G4-001`, the exact correspondence is:

```text
patch                         ↔ subset V ⊆ U
local observer section        ↔ equivalence relation E_V ∈ EqRel(V)
pairwise overlap agreement    ↔ equality after restriction
generated closure E*          ↔ least equivalence generated by local equalities
exact global G4 gluing        ↔ an amalgam with exact restrictions
```

Here `EqRel(V)` is the set of equivalence relations on `V`, with restriction
along subset inclusions. When the local family is pairwise compatible, it is a
matching family for this set-valued presheaf. The G4 contradiction criterion
decides whether that matching family has an exact amalgam.

This is **not** a claim that Veyra implements a general sheaf, Grothendieck
topology, effective descent, stack, or category-theoretic equivalence. G4
handles one supplied finite cover/family and distinguishes existence from
uniqueness.

## Quotient conflict classification

Assume an exact gluing exists. Let

```text
Q = U / E*
```

and connect two distinct classes of `Q` when one patch contains representatives
from both. Such an edge is a **conflict**: merging the two classes would add an
equality inside that patch and violate exact restriction.

Every exact global gluing is obtained by partitioning `Q` into blocks that
contain no conflict edge, and lifting that quotient partition back to `U`.
Conversely every conflict-safe partition lifts to an exact gluing. Therefore:

```text
exact gluings ↔ conflict-safe partitions of Q
unique exact gluing ↔ the conflict graph is complete
```

The production classifier hard-gates this construction on exact existence. In
particular, the pairwise-compatible G4 triangle is nongluable and exposes no
spurious quotient gluing.

## Exact finite verification

`scripts/verify_g4_gluing.py` is an independent oracle: it imports no production
G4 classifier, brute-forces all global equivalence relations, separately builds
`E*`, and checks the quotient bijection. With real SageMath 10.7 it additionally
cross-checks every local partition through `SetPartitions`.

The canonical exhaustive scope uses `U={0,…,n-1}` and unordered sets of distinct
nonempty patch carriers whose union is `U`:

| `n` | cover shapes | assignments | matching | gluable | unique | gluing-count histogram |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | `{1: 1}` |
| 2 | 5 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 8 | `{1: 8, 2: 1}` |
| 3 | 109 | 1265 | 505 | 481 | 432 | `{0: 784, 1: 432, 2: 36, 3: 12, 5: 1}` |

Totals are `115` cover shapes, `1275` local assignments, `515` matching
families, `491` gluable families, `441` uniquely gluable families, and `556`
global witnesses. Twenty-four matching families at `n=3` remain nongluable.

Named duplicate carriers have a separate reduction boundary: unequal local
sections disagree on their full overlap and block gluing, while equal sections
can be deduplicated without changing exact global relations, `E*`, the conflict
graph, or uniqueness. The table itself counts only distinct-carrier covers; it
does not count arbitrary naming multiplicities or claim the externally reported
random `n=4` sample.

Run the real-Sage lane with:

```bash
sage -python scripts/verify_g4_gluing.py --max-nodes 3 --require-sage
```

## Strict predicate separation

`SEP-G4-001` records one exact finite separation:

```text
“an exact gluing exists”  ≠  “a unique exact gluing exists”.
```

Two disjoint singleton patches admit both the identity relation and the
universal relation as exact global gluings. The relations are extensionally
different. This witnesses predicate non-equivalence only; it says nothing
about whether other formalisms can express either predicate.

## Lean and certificate boundary

The existing three registered theorem cards remain `THM_G4_001..003`. The same
digest-bound Lean file now contains two nonpromoted helpers:

- pair coverage outside `E*` implies extensional uniqueness of exact gluing;
- disjoint singleton patches have two distinct exact gluings.

The full quotient-partition bijection is executable and exhaustively checked
through `n≤3`; it is not claimed as a promoted Lean theorem. Certificate
`comparative_bridge_separation_ledgers` adds one level-1 suite row, taking the
current suite from 101 to 102 without changing the 42-file Lean inventory.
