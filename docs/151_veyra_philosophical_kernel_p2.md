## 1. Purpose
Veyra must not confuse a successful observer test with being, a replayable construction with an object, or indefinite continuation with a completed infinity. Its philosophical kernel therefore has four layers:
| Layer | Governing question | Typical contents |
|---|---|---|
| ontology | what may become or be adopted? | rez, coupling, continuation, formation, completion principles |
| semantics | what do processes and responses mean? | observer, echo, translation, obstruction, typed silence |
| epistemology | what is justified by which source? | witness, provenance, scope, support, refutation, openness |
| QA | was the executable claim implemented faithfully? | replay, tests, certificates, coverage, mutation pressure |
A QA failure can defeat a certificate claim. It cannot by itself prove that an ontological postulate is false. Conversely, an adopted postulate does not make an implementation correct.
## 2. Observerhood is an acquired role
An observer is not primitive and is not defined as an arbitrary function. A
formation acquires an observer-role, relative to a declared coupling scope,
when all of the following are supplied:
1. a genealogy from admitted primitive operations;
2. structural closure and recurrence sufficient for another coupling;
3. discrimination: at least two allowed couplings leave different residues;
4. persistence: the discriminating trace survives a declared continuation;
5. efficacy: the retained difference can condition a later transition;
6. an explicit doctrine-level admission principle.
Call the last step the **Observer Emergence Principle** (`OEP`). It is a
sufficient recognition rule, not the cause or a biconditional definition of an
event. P1-E1 implements one finite formal shadow. The partial executable P3-OG
slice adds nonce-free deterministic pool-selection metadata, retained internal
residue and a finite maintenance-control pressure comparison. A separate
authority-free raw-cycle lane starts `UNFORMED` and replays the least nontrivial
return, but it is not a typed history or full formation judgment. P3-OG still
does not model historical blind/one-shot selection, typed post-formation
ablation, or the theorem obligations needed for an endogenous role.
Stateless maps and physical/conscious claims remain outside.
There is no final observer of all observers. A process may observe an encoded
observer, but that new coupling is itself scoped. Regress is stopped by finite
admission obligations and revisable provenance, not by a privileged viewpoint.
### 2.1 Role is not historical birth

OEP establishes capability and doctrine-relative role, not that a token arose.
The released **Historical Actualization Principle** (`HAP`) requires a unique
first-lineage birth cut, strict parent-derived Past/Future, raw P1-B+E1 replay,
target-sealed anti-circular strict-past closure, three mandatory counterfactual
classes, and same-token/lineage/scope post-birth efficacy. It separates birth
core, historical token, history, and judgment digests. Its `ESTABLISHED` claim is
only relative to the supplied finite history; physical/preformal birth,
consciousness, absolute causation, and observer-independent actuality remain
unclaimed.

## 3. Observers form a typed translation network

Two observers are related only through declared source/response transports and
domains. Same-source refinement fixes source transport to identity/inclusion;
for its partial response map `tau : response(f) -> response(c)`, distinguish:

- **preservation:** translated fine agreement implies coarse agreement;
- **reflection:** coarse agreement implies fine agreement on the comparison
  domain;
- **domain coverage:** every coarse-defined input is fine-defined;
- **information loss:** distinct fine responses can translate to one coarse
  response;
- **incomparability:** both relation inclusions are freshly refuted by distinct
  crossing witnesses; absence of a map is only missing translation evidence;
- **conflict:** a claimed translation diagram freshly fails.

A2 strict refinement requires preservation established, reflection refuted,
and equal ready domains on the exact scope; equivalence requires both implications
and domain equality established. Structural P1-A factorization/coverage remains
separate evidence, and finite sample agreement is not a general law. Translation
composition and identity must replay under the same source/doctrine commitments.

This network answers how observers can compare results without pretending that
they share a view from nowhere. Its judgments remain relation- and
scope-relative.

P3-T realizes one exact finite P1-bound network; P3-N2 separately realizes one
thin arithmetic reduction network. Rereview-pending P3-TG requires typed source maps/equivalences,
nonvacuous commuting response maps, silence action, path cells and separating
descent before scoped objectivity. Neither released network yields a universal
observer order, ontic identity, or observer-independent objectivity.

## 4. Existence is a typed profile
Veyra does not use “exists” as an untyped Boolean. At minimum it distinguishes:
| Judgment | Positive content |
|---|---|
| presented | a representation is supplied |
| admissible | it satisfies a named doctrine |
| observable | an admitted coupling returns a typed response |
| constructible-in | a closed operation or exact foundation realization rule is available |
| generable | a bound process freshly replays it |
| denoted-in | an exact semantic rule assigns content to the occurrence/presentation |
| consistent-premise-slice | its exact formation premises are conflict-free or proved isolated |
| coherent | every declared compatibility obligation passes |
| persistent | named continuation witnesses preserve its trace |
| stable | declared alternative paths reconcile |
| refinement-robust | demanded genuine refinements preserve the formation |
| witnessed | replayable evidence supports the judgment |
| scoped object | a named formation rule adopts the structured presentation |
These judgments are not one linear ladder. A silent response may be a positive
observation. A supplied presentation may be non-generable. A generable path may
be nonconfluent. A coherent finite family need not have a completed carrier.

## 5. Stability is reconciliation of histories

Echo says that responses agree. Persistence says that one history transports a
trace. Neither says that different histories constitute one stable formation.

Stability requires a separate confluence obligation:

1. histories start from the same bound stage;
2. every edge and response is freshly replayed;
3. their endpoints are joined or the histories close against an explicit
   zero-edge identity history;
4. the full transport diagram commutes, directly or through a named bridge;
5. every path pair or cycle demanded by the finite scope is covered;
6. the first obstruction remains visible.

One joined fork gives fork confluence only. A finite catalog can establish
`GLOBAL_DECLARED_FINITE_CONFLUENT` only for that exact catalog. It cannot imply
Church–Rosser confluence, unique normal forms, or unbounded path independence.

Under `SFP`, confluence explains why branch residues belong to one admitted
formation: not because names or endpoint hashes coincide, but because their
genealogies are reconciled by the declared transport structure.

## 6. Scoped objectivity

Observer relativity does not force arbitrary subjectivism. Objectivity is an
invariance claim over a named system of transformations:

> A law is objectively stable relative to doctrine `D`, scope `S`, and
> refinement family `R` when it is preserved through every admitted translation
> and genuine refinement in `R`, every required diagram commutes, and no bound
> conflict exists.

This is stronger than coincidental agreement by several weak observers because
the translations, domains, losses, and refinement relations are themselves
part of the evidence. It remains weaker than observer-free truth. Expanding the
doctrine may split an old echo class; the previous result was scoped, not false.

The route from relativity to objectivity is therefore:

```text
single response
  -> observer-indexed echo
  -> translated multi-observer agreement
  -> path confluence
  -> nonvacuous refinement robustness
  -> doctrine-scoped objectivity
```

No finite point on this route licenses “all conceivable observers.”

## 7. The ontology of silence

Every absence-like outcome must carry a constructor:

| Constructor | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RESPONSE_SILENT` | the observer ran and returned its silent mark |
| `INTRINSIC_SILENT` | the process is the native silence form |
| `OPERATIONALLY_ABSENT` | no event occurred in a declared finite window |
| `DOMAIN_BLOCKED` | the operation is not defined on this input |
| `OBSTRUCTED` | incompatible evidence or a failed obligation exists |
| `RESOURCE_LIMITED` | evaluation stopped before semantic judgment |
| `UNOBSERVED` | no admitted observation was performed |
| `EPISTEMICALLY_OPEN` | present evidence supports neither side |
| `UNRESOLVED_IN_SYSTEM` | a named formal system proves neither side, when established |
| `DIVERGENT` | execution exceeds its termination contract |

Only the first three contain positive silence/absence content, and even they
are relative to a response kind, process form, or finite window. None of the
others may be coerced into nonexistence.
## 8. Three infinities, not one
Veyra separates three strengths:
1. **potential infinity:** one fixed productive rule can answer each separately
   demanded finite depth;
2. **all-depth family:** one extensional compatible family has a component at
   every finite depth;
3. **completed infinity:** a separately constructed carrier realizes such
   families and a bound witness establishes unbounded/no-greatest indexing.
The first does not imply the second merely because arbitrarily large tests pass.
The **All-depth Family Introduction Principle** (`AFIP`) may introduce one family
only from an accepted totality basis. Candidate A1c requires a closed total
`eval_G`, allowlisted constructive basis, exact runs, determinism and coherence, then defines
`F_G:=eval_G`; weak pointwise productivity still cannot supply that term.
The second does not imply the third. `PΩ` must name carrier, projections,
realization, separation and ledger. Task-local `RCIΩ` additionally requires an
exact no-greatest or strict-cofinal index source, its bound projections and
foundation/size ledger for `CompletedInfinity(P,J)`; no carrier release claims it.
New-information witnesses and carrier cardinality remain separate theorems.
The first positive model releases `Stream(A) = Nat -> A`: diagonal evaluation
realizes every compatible finite-prefix family and joint separation gives relative
uniqueness, only under the exact doctrine/ledger. Focused `37/37`, isolated Lean
`15/15`, ledger `36/46` with `Quot.sound`, registry `89`, and the exact assumption ledger recorded in `THEOREMS.md`.
This establishes neither generic inverse limits nor physical, metaphysical, or
foundation-independent actual infinity. The non-finite step is imported, not
manufactured: `Stream(A) = Nat -> A` presupposes Lean's completed inductive
`Nat`, and every released `forall n` quantifies with the host recursor, so the
theorems transfer admitted actual infinity from the metatheory — exactly as the
N6C boundary states for its `CompletedNat` premise; nothing here completes
finite Veyra modes into an index totality. PΩ2 is a separate exact-ledger prime-power carrier; D1/D3 promote into neither.
## 9. Consequence for number theory
The separation is especially useful for prime-power residue towers.
- A bounded list of residues is finite evidence.
- A uniform rule producing the residue modulo `p^n` is a productive process.
- A compatible residue at every `n` is an all-depth family.
- A p-adic integer is obtained only after adopting the relevant inverse-limit
  carrier and its projection/universal obligations.
Thus “the same number at every precision” is not primitive identity. It is a
coherent family whose finite shadows translate and restrict consistently. A
completed p-adic object is an additional construction, not a poetic synonym
for endless calculation.
This gives number theory a precise experimental agenda: search for intrinsic
generators, prove compatibility, pressure confluence and refinement, then state
exactly which completion turns the family into an object. Classical results may
serve as declared semantic bridges; their names cannot substitute for those
steps.
The released number-theoretic completion makes the commitment explicit:
`ZpVeyra(p)` is the subtype of all compatible residues modulo `p^(n+1)`. Its
carrier-relative realization is structurally simple because the carrier is the
compatible-family presentation itself. The non-finite step is nevertheless real: the
foundation must admit a completed dependent function over every `n`, its full
compatibility proposition, subtype formation, and extensionality. PΩ2 therefore
locates an exact doctrine-relative completed carrier in an exposed ledger instead of smuggling it through the
phrase “continue forever”; see `152_padic_completion_pomega2.md`.

## 11. What remains genuinely open
Even after the finite rules are implemented, the philosophical kernel will not
have settled:
- physical and preformal emergence of observers;
- doctrine choice independent of historical investigators;
- completeness of any observer/refinement family;
- absolute identity or observer-independent objecthood;
- higher-cell C2.3 coherence and any universal/unrestricted confluence theorem;
- a generic conversion of productive rules into extensional families;
- a universal completion or unrestricted actual infinity; PΩ1/PΩ2 are exact-ledger carriers only;
- consciousness, physical continua, and transfinite ontology.
These are boundaries of the current doctrine, not failures disguised as silence. Veyra advances by giving each stronger claim its own constructor, source, obstruction language, and proof burden.
